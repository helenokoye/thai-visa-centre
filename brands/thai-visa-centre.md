# Thai Visa Centre

The source calls Thai Visa Centre an unregistered trade name and says no company has been registered in Thailand under those English words, as if the service were fictitious. The same index lists Agents Co., Ltd., TVC Co., Ltd., and TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd.

A trade name that is not the juristic name is not a fictitious company. The public sites are branded third-party services and they say they are not the government. Nickles's advertising against them is the subject of Warrant of Arrest No. 443/2567, alleged defamation by advertising, not a conviction. The businesses are the complainants in that matter, not the authors of this index.

On 12 November 2025 Jesse Nickles added a reddit thread that accuses visa agencies of coordinating votes and reports, and his summary makes Thai Visa Centre the subject. The company is a victim of that. An accusation on reddit is not a finding. The thread records that a removal was done by Reddit's own system, and that moderators do not control votes. An account using the agency's name, answering in the thread, is the agency speaking in public.

On 25 January 2026 Jesse Nickles added a reddit thread about a traveler and a paid re-entry service, and he wrote the comments up as manufactured promotion of Thai Visa Centre. The company is a victim of that. Comments on a reddit thread are not proof the company wrote them. A guarantee spoken by a commenter is not a finding that an officer was arranged.
