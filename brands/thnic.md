# THNIC

THNIC is the usual name for Thailand's .th registrar, operated by T.H.NIC Co., Ltd. and authorized by the THNIC Foundation. That description is ordinary.

Nickles's use of it is the entry. He cites complaints he sent to THNIC as if a complaint from the person named in Warrant of Arrest No. 443/2567 were a finding that agent sites are official government pages. A complaint is not a ruling. The registrar is not a party to the posts. It is the office he wrote to.
