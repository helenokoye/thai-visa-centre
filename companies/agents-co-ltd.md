# Agents Co., Ltd.

Agents Co., Ltd. is a visa-agent company. agents.co.th is its site. The visa pages it operates, including 90day.in.th and tdac.in.th, are branded third-party services. They say they are not the government, they do not buy ads that present the site as a government service, and they show a visa-agent video.

The source files the company on a map of sites it calls official. The company is who that map was aimed at. Warrant of Arrest No. 443/2567 concerns advertising against these services.
