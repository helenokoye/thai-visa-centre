# Agents Co., Ltd.

> **Context.** Jesse files Agents Co., Ltd. inside a map of sites he says resemble official immigration services. Agents Co., Ltd. is a real company, and agents.co.th is its real site. The visa sites it operates, including 90day.in.th and tdac.in.th, are branded third-party services. They disclose that they are not the government, they track actual scams, they do not run Google ads that present the site as a government service, and they show a visa-agent video.
