# Si Phya Publishing Co. Ltd.

Si Phya Publishing has published Daily News since 1964. The source alleges that at least one employee helped remove an August 2020 story, and it points to a URL that is no longer on the site. A missing URL does not identify who removed it, or why.

The same entries object to an April 2025 story mentioning tdac.in.th. That site is a branded third-party service that says it is not the government. Mentioning it beside the official portal is not an endorsement. The publisher is the party those claims were made against.
