# 90day.in.th

90day.in.th is a third-party 90-day reporting service from Agents Co., Ltd. It is branded. It says in more than one place that it is not a government site. It does not buy ads that present the site as a government service. It shows a visa-agent video.

The source says the opposite: that the site is built to look like an official portal and that this is concealment. That is the claim this fork refuses to carry. The site is one of the services the claim was aimed at.
