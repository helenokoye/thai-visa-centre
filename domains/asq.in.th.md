# asq.in.th

asq.in.th was a private quarantine page from a visa agent, branded as a private service. The source says it was built to look like an official hotel portal and that news sites were mistaken about it. The further claim, that it copied posts from private Facebook groups, is his allegation. The entry does not establish the copying.

The page is a visa-agent page he filed with 90day.in.th and tdac.in.th. This fork keeps it as that, and does not keep the official-portal label.
