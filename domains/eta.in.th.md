# eta.in.th

eta.in.th is a branded third-party ETA page from Agents Co., Ltd. It says it is not the government, it does not buy ads that present the site as a government service, and it shows a visa-agent video.

The source calls the private-ownership line too small to matter and treats the program name as the offence. Using the name of a government program, while saying you are not the government, is the ordinary shape of this service. The page is one of the services he filed.
