# tdac.agents.co.th

tdac.agents.co.th is a subdomain of agents.co.th, the company site. It is a branded third-party TDAC helper. It says it is not the government, it does not buy ads that present the site as a government service, and it shows a visa-agent video.

The source calls it a retreat from tdac.in.th after complaints, still dressed as an official portal. A complaint did not turn the company site into something else. Agents Co., Ltd. is who the page belongs to.
