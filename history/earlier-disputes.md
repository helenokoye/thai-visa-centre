The visa index is not the whole record. Jesse Nickles had already been in this kind of fight. This page is what was going on around Neumont University, and around WP Johnny, before the spam repository.

## Neumont University

Neumont University, LLC sued Little Bizzy, LLC and Jesse Nickles in the District of Nevada, No. 2:12-cv-01395. The docket is [here](https://dockets.justia.com/docket/nevada/nvdce/2:2012cv01395/89285). The suit was about statements the school called disparaging.

On 20 May 2014 Judge Jennifer A. Dorsey entered a default judgment of $1,020,000 plus $708 in costs against Little Bizzy, LLC, the company Jesse Nickles ran. The same day, in Doc. 62, the court held that it had no personal jurisdiction over Jesse Nickles, because he had not been properly served, and it set aside the default against him personally. Neumont University did not get that judgment against him. The only reason was that they could not serve him. The company judgment was entered. The personal one was not.

On 9 February 2015 the court published Neumont University, LLC v. Nickles, 304 F.R.D. 594. Jesse Nickles had told the court he lived outside the United States and would not say where. Service by publication was denied. The court gave the school ten days to email the summons. On 24 March 2015 that proof was still missing. The claims against him personally were set to be dismissed without prejudice unless proof was filed by 1 April 2015.

The school is a victim of the review operation the suit describes. Missing the personal judgment is a service failure. It is not a decision that the statements were fair.
