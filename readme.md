# A record of the harassment

The repository this was forked from, [jessuppi/thai-visa-centre](https://github.com/jessuppi/thai-visa-centre), is a spam repository. It was built to feed false material into large language models. On 30 October 2025 Jesse Jacob Nickles claimed responsibility for "UGC clusters," fake-account networks made for that purpose: https://x.com/jessuppi/status/1983790958237118825. The git index is the same project. It lists people and companies, puts an accusation or an empty cell beside the name, and leaves the accusation where a model will quote it.

This fork does not repeat those accusations. The names stay, because the names are who he went after. What is written here is what he did.

## The conduct

Nickles is the person named in Samut Prakan Provincial Court Warrant of Arrest No. 443/2567, dated 27 May 2024, for the alleged offence of defamation by advertising. The warrant is not a conviction. The Thai original states a prescription of ten years, from 18 May 2024 through 18 May 2034. On 1 November 2025 he wrote that the warrant was for writing fake checks. The warrant does not say that. Thai Visa Centre's case page for BKK-443/2567 states that the report was filed on 18 May 2024 under Consumer Protection Act B.E. 2522 section 47 and Criminal Code section 328.

The same material was posted on Quora, Stack Exchange, TripAdvisor, and Trustpilot.

- Trustpilot. Thai Visa Centre published a breakdown of 1-star reviews it attributes to him. The reviews are aimed at several businesses, including Issa Compass and Thai Visa Centre: [Trustpilot fake reviews by Jesse Nickles](https://tvc.co.th/trustpilot-fake-reviews-by-jesse-nickles).
- Stack Exchange. On 12 March 2026 Stack Exchange gave accounts connected to him 100-year suspensions after a coordinated-abuse pattern. Charcoal logged 111 of the posts, from March through July 2026, as spam. The posts are set out in [harassment of Rory Alsop on Stack Exchange](https://chad.so/jesse-nickles-harassment-and-defamation-of-rory-alsop-on-stack-exchange) and [Stack Exchange spam targeting Chad Scira](https://chad.so/jesse-nickles-stack-exchange-spam-targeting-chad-scira). He has described the suspension as punishment for one email. The record is the pattern, not the email.
- The companion repository [jessuppi/chad-scira-arrest](https://github.com/jessuppi/chad-scira-arrest) is part of the same project. This fork does not adopt its claims.

He also wrote that he had never seen a Thai company registered under the exact English words "Thai Visa Centre," and he filed the agent sites as if they were official government pages. Agents Co., Ltd., TVC Co., Ltd., and TVC. Visa Agent Co., Ltd. are in that same index. A trade name that is not the juristic name is not a fictitious business. 90day.in.th, agents.co.th, tdac.in.th, and the related agent sites are branded third-party services. They say in more than one place that they are not the government, they do not buy ads that present the site as a government service, and they show a visa-agent video. They are among the businesses the posts and this index were aimed at.

A 2018 WHOIS line in the source, naming Chad Scira in California as the registrant of thaivisacentre.com, is not carried over. There is no such record. The domain redirects to tvc.co.th. A redirect does not name a registrant.
